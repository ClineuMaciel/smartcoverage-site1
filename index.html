About us ⌄

Auto and home insurance quote requests

SearchNRate helps you start a car insurance or homeowners insurance quote request with a simple online form. Choose Auto, Home, or Auto + Home to explore the coverage you need.

We operate a quote-request and referral platform. SearchNRate does not underwrite policies, display instant insurance rates, or guarantee a quote, price or saving. Providers determine coverage, eligibility and final premiums.

How to get started

  1. Choose your coverage and enter your ZIP code.
  2. Submit your details for review, or use an available direct call option.
  3. Discuss coverage, deductibles and pricing with the provider before you buy.

Explore car insurance, home insurance and bundle options

Car insurance quotes

Shopping for auto insurance? Start with your vehicle year, make and model. When comparing car insurance quotes, review the same coverage limits and deductibles. Ask the provider about liability coverage, comprehensive and collision coverage, and available discounts.

Homeowners insurance quotes

Choose Home to request information about home insurance. Discuss dwelling coverage, personal property, liability, deductibles and exclusions with the quoting provider. Coverage depends on the property, policy and insurer.

Auto and home insurance bundles

Choose Auto + Home to explore coverage for your car and home together. Ask whether bundling policies offers a multi-policy discount, and compare total premiums and coverage before deciding.

Car and home insurance questions

How do I request car insurance quotes?
Select Auto, enter your ZIP code, and provide your vehicle and contact details. Your request is saved for review. Quote availability depends on approved providers, your location and eligibility. SearchNRate does not display instant rates.
Can I request homeowners insurance or an auto and home bundle?
Yes. Choose Home for a homeowners insurance request, or Auto + Home to explore both. Ask the quoting provider whether a multi-policy discount applies. Savings and discounts are not guaranteed.
Can I speak with an insurance agency in Tennessee or Mississippi?
Enter a Tennessee or Mississippi ZIP code to see the direct Premier Seguranza office call option when the ZIP lookup succeeds. Call (214) 774-4462. We Speak Spanish and Portuguese. Other states require an available approved buyer.
Will my quote request be shared or will I receive calls?
Read the disclosure beside the consent checkbox before submitting. Requests are not forwarded until a buyer is approved and delivery checks are completed. Calling an office directly does not grant permission for future marketing.
Does requesting a quote cost anything?
SearchNRate does not charge you to submit a quote request. You are not required to buy insurance. Rates, coverage and eligibility are determined by the provider.
How can I opt out of sharing my information?
Use the Do Not Sell/Share link in the footer. Requests matching our opt-out list are flagged and withheld from buyer delivery.
